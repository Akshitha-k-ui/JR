FROM TEACHER TO PARTNER ❤️ — Birthday Story Website
Birthday: 22 October 2026

OPEN IT
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. The website runs locally. Internet is only needed for optional Google Fonts.

ADD YOUR PHOTOS
Copy your images into media/photos and use these filenames (JPG recommended):
hero.jpg, birthday.jpg, classroom.jpg, keychain.jpg, diary.jpg, scooty.jpg,
recovery.jpg, chats.jpg, train.jpg, bangalore.jpg, beach.jpg, night-out.jpg,
first-kiss.jpg, reconciliation.jpg, ramadan.jpg, airport.jpg, videocall.jpg, us.jpg.
If a photo is missing, a placeholder remains. To change a chapter's photo path,
edit the `chapters` array at the top of script.js.

ADD VIDEO
Put your video at media/videos/our-story.mp4. The video player is already included.

ADD SONG
Put a song you own or have permission to use at media/music/our-song.mp3.
Tap “Music off” in the top-right to start it; browsers don't allow autoplay by default.

EDIT TEXT
Edit the chapter text in script.js. The birthday letter and final birthday words are in index.html.
Keep private photos on a device you trust, and only share the website with people you trust.

This is a complete interactive story website with all 16 chapters, gallery placeholders,
a video area, music toggle, personal birthday letter, secret reveal, and birthday finale.
It is a website presentation, not an automatically rendered 16-scene live-action film.


MOONLIT QUIZ + SECRET BIRTHDAY VIDEO
The site now has a moon-and-stars night-sky theme and a quiz that unlocks your birthday-wish video.

1. Open script.js in a text editor.
2. Find `const quizQuestions = [` near the bottom.
3. Fill each blank `question: ""` and `answer: ""`, for example:
   { question: "What nickname does Ashu call me?", answer: "mwth" }
4. You can change the questions, answers, and number of question objects. Each question must have an answer.
5. Put your birthday greeting video at `media/videos/birthday-wish.mp4`.
6. Open index.html and test every answer before sending the website to him.

Answers are checked without case sensitivity but otherwise must match. The questions are deliberately left blank for you to personalise. This is a playful surprise, not secure authentication: someone with access to the website files can inspect the answers.
