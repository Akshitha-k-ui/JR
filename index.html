A LETTER FROM AMMU

To my mwth,

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️

Sometimes I think about how our story began. You walked into KEONICS as my student, and I was simply your teacher. Neither of us knew that a classroom, a computer desk, and all those little conversations would become the beginning of something so important to me.

From the Neymar keychain and the diary pages, to the scooty ride, the days when I couldn't walk, our late-night conversations, and the surprise in Bangalore — every memory has a little piece of my heart in it.

We've had beautiful days, difficult days, misunderstandings, and moments when life made things complicated. But what matters to me is that we kept trying to understand each other. Our story was never perfect; it became special because it was ours.

Now there are miles between us, different routines, and nights when I wake up just to see your face on my phone. I wish I could be beside you today, hold your hand, and wish you in person. Until that day comes, I hope you can feel my love in every message, every call, and every little thing I do for you.

Wherever your career takes you, I will always be proud of you. I hope this new year of your life brings you peace, courage, success, and all the happiness your heart deserves.

Thank you for becoming more than a memory from my classroom. Thank you for becoming my person.

From teacher to partner. From KEONICS to a love that crossed countries. Miles apart, but always close to my heart.

Always yours,
Your Ammu ♡

My wish is simple…

One day, no screens.
No goodbyes.
Just us, together.

Until then, I'll keep choosing you — in every time zone, every ordinary day, and every tomorrow.
